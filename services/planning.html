What planning includes

Everything that needs to happen between your idea and the first day on site.

01

Concept design

Floor plans, elevations and 3D views that show what you're building and how it sits on your site, with a cost estimate at each stage.

02

Council submission drawings

SANS 10400 compliant drawings, energy calculations and fire plans, prepared and submitted to your municipality.

03

Structural engineering

Foundation, slab, roof and steel design by registered engineers, including site inspections during the build.

04

Soil & site surveys

Geotechnical tests and site surveys so foundation designs suit the clay, rock and water tables common in the Breede Valley.

05

Quantity surveying

Itemised bills of quantities, so the price you sign is based on measured materials, not guesswork.

06

Building programme

A week-by-week plan showing when each stage happens, so you can plan finance draws, moves and harvest dates.

How planning works

Four steps from a first conversation to approved plans and a fixed price.

  1. Brief & site visit

    We meet on site, look at slope, access and services, and agree on what you need and what you want to spend.

  2. Concept & estimate

    Concept drawings with a cost estimate. We adjust the design until it fits your budget.

  3. Approvals

    Final drawings, engineering and energy calculations submitted to the municipality. We answer comments and track progress.

  4. Fixed price

    Once plans are approved, you receive an itemised fixed-price quote and programme, ready to build.

Costs & timing

What affects plan approval times

Plan approval in the Boland usually takes 6 to 12 weeks after a complete submission. These six factors make it faster or slower.

  1. ZoningIf what you want to build doesn't match the property's zoning, a rezoning or consent-use application can add several months.
  2. Building lines and servitudesBuilding over a building line or servitude needs a relaxation, which means notifying your neighbours.
  3. HeritageStructures older than 60 years and properties in heritage areas need a heritage permit before alterations.
  4. A complete submissionIncomplete submissions are the main cause of delays. We submit complete packages the first time.
  5. Size and complexityLarger commercial and public buildings need more engineering detail and a fire-safety review.
  6. Municipal workloadApproval times vary by municipality and time of year. We track every submission and follow up weekly.

Planning questions

How long does building plan approval take in Worcester?

Breede Valley Municipality usually takes 6 to 12 weeks after a complete submission for standard residential plans. Larger or non-standard applications can take longer.

Do I need an architect if I use Boland Structures?

No. Our planning team includes registered architectural professionals who draw and sign off your plans. If you already have an architect, we're happy to work with them.

Is the site visit really free?

Yes. The first site visit and discussion is free, with no obligation. Design and drawing work is quoted separately once you decide to go ahead.

Do I pay for planning if I don't build with you?

Planning work is quoted as a separate fee. You own the approved plans and can use any builder, but most clients build with us because the price is already fixed.

What is SANS 10400?

SANS 10400 is South Africa's national building regulations standard. It sets the rules for structure, fire safety, energy efficiency, drainage and more, and every plan must comply before it's approved.

Can you get plans approved for an existing house?

Yes. If your house has unapproved additions, we draw as-built plans and submit them for approval. You'll need these before you can sell.
