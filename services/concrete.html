Concrete works

Concrete done right the first time

Boland Structures is a concrete contractor in Worcester, pouring foundations, slabs, industrial floors, retaining walls and water-retaining structures across the Boland. Our own crew does every pour, every pour is cube-tested, and every structural element is signed off by an engineer.

Concrete is the one part of a building you can't fix later. A badly compacted foundation or a slab poured on wet clay cracks for years. We test the ground, check every steel fixing before the pour, and cure concrete properly. In the Boland's hot summers that means wet-curing and shading, not hoping for the best.

We do concrete work as part of our own builds, and as a standalone service for other contractors, farmers and businesses.

Costs & timing

What affects the cost of concrete work

Concrete work is priced on the volume of ready-mix plus labour, steel and formwork. These six factors change the total.

  1. Volume and thicknessThe amount of concrete is the biggest cost. Industrial floors and raft foundations need far more than a standard house slab.
  2. ReinforcementSteel mesh, rebar and fibres add strength, and cost, depending on the engineer's design.
  3. Access and pumpingIf a ready-mix truck can't reach the pour, we need a concrete pump, which adds a daily hire cost.
  4. Ground preparationClay soils may need to be dug out and replaced with compacted fill before a slab can be poured.
  5. FinishPower-floating, polishing, sealing or acid-resistant coatings cost more than a plain finish.
  6. Distance from the batching plantReady-mix has to be placed within a set time. Remote farm sites may need on-site mixing or retarders.

Concrete questions

How thick should a concrete slab be?

A standard house floor slab is usually 85–100 mm on a prepared base. Garage and industrial floors are 150 mm or more. Your engineer specifies the exact thickness and reinforcement.

How long does concrete take to cure?

You can usually walk on concrete after 24–48 hours, and it reaches most of its design strength in 28 days. We keep it moist for at least 7 days, especially in Boland summer heat.

Do you do concrete work for other builders in Worcester?

Yes. As a concrete contractor we pour foundations, slabs and industrial floors for other builders, and directly for farmers and businesses.

Why do concrete slabs crack?

Most cracks come from poor ground preparation, missing control joints or concrete drying out too fast. Proper compaction, joints and wet-curing prevent most of them.

Can you pour concrete on a remote farm?

Yes. We plan ready-mix deliveries around travel times, and use pumps or on-site mixing when trucks can't reach the pour.

Do you test the concrete?

Yes. We take cube samples from every structural pour and test them at 7 and 28 days. The results go into your handover file.
