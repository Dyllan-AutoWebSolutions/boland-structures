Building in Worcester: what to know

Four things that shape almost every Worcester build, and how we handle them.

  1. Summer heatWorcester regularly passes 38 °C in summer. We pour concrete before 10:00, wet-cure it for 7 days and shade fresh slabs so they don't crack.
  2. Clay near the riverPlots closer to the Hex and Breede rivers often sit on expansive clay. We soil-test before quoting and price the right foundation from day one.
  3. Breede Valley approvalsStandard residential plans take 6 to 12 weeks after a complete submission. We submit complete packages and follow up weekly.
  4. Older town-centre buildingsBuildings older than 60 years around Church Street and the town centre may need a heritage permit before alterations.
