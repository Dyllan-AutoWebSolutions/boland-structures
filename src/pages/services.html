Common questions

Which areas do you work in?

The whole Boland and Breede River Valley: Worcester, Rawsonville, De Doorns, Robertson, Ceres, Wellington and Paarl. Further afield for larger commercial projects.

Is the quote really fixed?

Yes. The price on your signed quote is the price you pay. The only time it changes is when you ask for a change, and you approve the cost in writing before we start that work.

Do you handle council approvals?

We prepare and submit plans to the local municipality, follow up on comments and manage inspections. You sign the forms; we do the running around.

Can we live in the house during a renovation?

In most cases, yes. We phase the work so you always have a working kitchen and bathroom, seal off work areas and only work 07:00–17:00 on weekdays.

What does the 5-year guarantee cover?

Any defect in our workmanship: structural, roofing, waterproofing and finishes. If something we built fails, we come back and fix it at no cost.
