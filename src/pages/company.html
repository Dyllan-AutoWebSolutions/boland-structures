A family-run Worcester contractor with 22 years of homes, cellars, warehouses and schools behind us.

22Years building

From farm sheds to wine cellars

Boland Structures started in 2004 with two bakkies, one cement mixer and a contract for a packing shed in the Hex Valley.

Farmers kept calling back because we finished before harvest and the price never moved. The packing sheds led to cellars, the cellars led to homes, and the homes led to schools, clinics and business parks across the Breede River Valley.

Today we run a crew of 85 out of our yard in Worcester. We are still family-owned and we still answer the phone ourselves.

What we stand for

Four rules every person on a Boland Structures site works by.

01

Safety first

Every crew member goes home in one piece. Daily toolbox talks and a full-time safety officer on every site.

02

Honest pricing

The quote you sign is the amount you pay. If something changes, you approve it in writing first.

03

Local crews

91% of our team lives within 40 km of Worcester. We train apprentices from the communities we build in.

04

Finish strong

A building isn't finished until the snag list is empty. Every job is backed by a 5-year workmanship guarantee.
