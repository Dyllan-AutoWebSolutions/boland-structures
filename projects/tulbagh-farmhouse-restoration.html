Tulbagh Farmhouse Restoration

The project

The brief

New owners of a Cape Dutch farmhouse outside Tulbagh wanted to live in it full-time, with modern bathrooms and a kitchen, without losing its gables, yellowwood ceilings and thick walls.

The challenge

The house is over 200 years old, so every change needed a heritage permit. Decades of cement plaster had trapped rising damp in the original walls.

Our solution

Our planning team prepared the heritage application with a registered heritage practitioner and had the permit in ten weeks. We stripped the cement plaster, let the walls dry and re-plastered with breathable lime. Four bathrooms were fitted into existing rooms, so no original walls were removed.
