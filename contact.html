Before you get in touch

How quickly will you reply?

Within one working day, usually the same day. Quote requests get a call from a project manager to book a site visit.

Do you work outside Worcester?

Yes. We build across the Boland and Breede River Valley, including Rawsonville, De Doorns, Robertson, Ceres, Tulbagh, Wellington and Paarl.

Is the first site visit free?

Yes. We'll meet you on site, walk the ground and talk through your project at no cost and with no obligation.

I'm a supplier or subcontractor. Who do I contact?

Use the form above and choose "Supplier or subcontractor". Our procurement team keeps an approved list and reviews it every quarter.

Need a price?

Our quote form takes about 3 minutes. You'll get a fixed-price quote within 5 working days of your site visit.
