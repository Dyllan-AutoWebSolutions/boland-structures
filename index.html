About Boland

A Worcester-based contractor building homes, cellars, warehouses and schools across the Breede River Valley for over two decades.

Two bakkies, one cement mixer, 2004

We started with farm sheds and boundary walls in the Hex Valley. Twenty-two years later we run a crew of 85 and deliver projects from Ceres to Robertson, but we still answer the phone ourselves.
